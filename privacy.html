Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Summary: FPS Meter does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What the app reads on your phone

  • Frame timing of the apps you choose (through Shizuku), to calculate FPS
  • CPU load, memory use, battery level and temperature, and the temperature sensors your phone makes available
  • The list of installed apps, only so you can choose which ones to measure. This list never leaves your phone.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Session history (game name, FPS statistics and graph points) and your settings, in the app's private storage
  • Optional: a CSV log in your Downloads folder, only if you turn on logging
  • Result images in Pictures/FpsMeter, only when you share a result

You can delete the history inside the app (History > Clear all) or by uninstalling the app.

Internet use

The app uses the internet only to check for app updates: it downloads a small version file from GitHub. Like any web request, GitHub can see your IP address. If you choose to update, the app downloads the new APK from GitHub. The app sends no personal data.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps: to show the overlay
  • Foreground service and notifications: to keep the overlay running and to show optional alerts
  • Internet: update check
  • Install packages: to install an update, only after you approve it
  • Shizuku access (granted inside Shizuku): to read frame data

Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/ahyakonbong5-create/Fps-meter/issues.
